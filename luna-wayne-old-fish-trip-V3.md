makes it feel like a real adventure, not just five postcards. The trip

story, voices and rubric score changed. See "What changed from V2 to V3"

## What changed from V2 to V3 — every change, nothing removed silently

1. **Title and story:** V3 is called "LUNA, WAYNE AND OLD FISH — The Great
   Christmas Road Trip". Each day has its own mood and colour: Day 1 warm
   desert orange, Day 2 golden sunrise, Day 3 white winter snow, Day 4
   snowy red hoodoos, Day 5 warm Christmas Eve sunset.
2. **Luna has a different face every day.** V2 Luna had almost the same
   face on Days 1–4. V3 Luna is excited on Day 1, amazed on Day 2,
   laughing on Day 3, full of wonder on Day 4, happy and warm on Day 5.
3. **They talk to each other now.** V2 they mostly posed. V3 has little
   interactions in the new script: Luna points and shouts "SNOW!",
   Wayne answers friendly, Old Fish says "Temperature approaching
   fish-stick conditions" and Luna says "OLD FISH, NO!".
4. **Wayne is friendly, NOT grumpy.** Stella disagreed with an outside
   review that called Wayne a grumpy navigator. V3 Wayne smiles, holds
   the map, looks after Old Fish's bowl, and says "I did not drop the
   bowl, not even once." This is now rule R8 in the rubric.
5. **Old Fish is the little scientist.** V3 Old Fish loves numbers: he
   counts the arch ("Bigger than one hundred fish bowls!"), counts the
   hoodoos, and calculates the trip costs at the end — about $1,450–
   $1,900 total, ESTIMATE, $80 park pass, $8 a person for Monument
   Valley, $10 a car for Horseshoe Bend, 100% joy.
6. **Christmas feeling.** V3 Luna and Wayne wear warm red Christmas
   scarves. Day 3, 4 and 5 have falling snow. Day 5 has a small wrapped
   Christmas gift and warm drinks in mugs, and a group photo at
   Horseshoe Bend — a memorable ending, not just another stop.
7. **Bigger scenery.** V3 makes the Mittens, the arches and the hoodoos
   enormous, with the characters small below them, so the places feel big.
8. **Old Fish in every panel — fixed by hand.** The first V3 strip left
   Old Fish out of Day 1 and Day 2, and had Wayne twice in Day 2, and
   captions in the wrong order. The second V3 strip fixed Wayne and the
   captions, but still left Old Fish out of Day 1 and Day 2. So his bowl
   was copied from the Day 5 panel and pasted in by hand, in a circle,
   on the car seat on Day 1 and on the rock on Day 2. Now he is in every
   panel. The Day 1 caption is written twice (above and below the panel)
   — a small flaw, named here honestly.
9. **Longer video, with the new story.** V3 video is 118 seconds, not
   64, because the friends talk to each other now. Same voices as V2:
   Narrator Aria, Luna Paloma, Wayne Miles, Old Fish Caitlin, all clean.
10. **Rubric 3.0.** V3 is scored against Rubric 3.0 in `RUBRIC.md`:
    Option B plus the Artist's Scorecard, rules R1–R8. The old Rubric
    2.0 is kept as `RUBRIC-2.0.md`.
11. **Removed from V2:** the same-face Luna, the posing-only strip, the
    silent back-view ending, and the 64-second video with no interactions.
    Those stay in GitHub history, but they are NOT V3.

## Day 1 — Saturday Dec 20, 2025: Phoenix → Monument Valley → Moab
**Drive:** about 7.5 hours total. **Stay:** Mainstay Suites, Moab — 356 S Main St, Building B, Moab, UT 84532. 3 beds.

**Spots, in order:**
- 07:30–12:30 Phoenix north, fuel and a quick lunch in Flagstaff or Kayenta.
- 12:30–14:45 Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park, about 2 hours. Photos of the Mittens from the Visitor Center terrace. If the road is dry and the car is high enough, the 17-mile Valley Drive. If not, the overlooks, about 45 minutes.
- 14:45–15:00 Forrest Gump Point on US-163, 15 minutes north. The long straight road photo.
- 15:00–17:30 Drive to Moab, about 2.5 hours. Check in, dinner, shop at City Market for tomorrow's picnic sandwiches and water. No water or gas inside Arches/Canyonlands in winter.

**Where to eat:** Lunch on the road in Flagstaff or Kayenta, quick and cheap. Dinner in Moab — Pasta Jay's, 4 S Main St, big pasta portions, family-friendly, or Moab Diner, 189 S Main St, open 6am–10pm, all-day breakfast and burgers. Both are on Main Street, a short walk from the hotel.

**Why stay here:** Mainstay Suites in Moab puts them in town for two nights, so no repacking on Day 2. Moab is the base for both Canyonlands and Arches. City Market is in town for picnic supplies.

**Photo (V1, Luna + Wayne only):** Panel 1 of the connected strip — Luna leaning out of the car window, Wayne once in the back seat, the Mittens ahead. Old Fish is missing — add him in V2.

**Vlog script, Day 1 — Luna:** "We left Phoenix at half past seven, and by lunch we were looking at the Mittens. They are so much bigger in real life. Wayne fell asleep in the back seat, and Old Fish kept saying 'are we there yet' at every red rock."
**Wayne:** "I was not asleep. I was resting my one good eye."
**Journal, Day 1:** First day, longest drive. Monument Valley felt like another planet — flat desert, then three huge rocks standing alone. Forrest Gump Point was just a road, but the photo is the best one so far. Moab at night, tired, full, ready for sunrise tomorrow at 6:40am.

## Day 2 — Sunday Dec 21, 2025: Canyonlands + Arches, Moab
**Drive:** about 1.5 hours in the parks. **Stay:** Holiday Inn Express & Suites Moab — 1515 North Highway 191, Moab, UT 84532. Second night in Moab.

**Spots, in order:**
- 06:40–09:30 Canyonlands, Island in the Sky. Drive 40 minutes from Moab. Mesa Arch at 7:20am sunrise, easy 15-minute flat walk. Then Grand View Point.
- 10:00–17:30 Arches National Park. No timed-entry reservation in 2026 in the real plan; in the story, just show the pass.
- Morning: Windows Section — North and South Windows, Double Arch — and Balanced Rock. Picnic lunch.
- About 14:00: Delicate Arch Trail, about 3 miles round trip, 2–2.5 hours with a child, walking slowly. Afternoon sun on the arch, stay for sunset, then back to Moab for dinner.

**Where to eat:** Breakfast early at Moab Diner, open at 6am. Lunch is the picnic from City Market, eaten in the car or at a picnic area. Dinner in Moab — The Broken Oar, 53 W 400 N, barbecue, burgers and sweet potato fries, lodge-style and good after a long hike, or Pasta Jay's again if everyone is tired.

**Why stay here:** Second night in the same town, same hotel area, so Day 2 starts early without packing. Holiday Inn Express is on the north side of Moab, on the road to Arches, and it is close to the parks for the 6:40am start.

**Photo (V1):** Panel 2 — Luna in the middle at Mesa Arch / Delicate Arch country at sunset, Wayne once on a rock on the right.

**Vlog script, Day 2 — Luna:** "Mesa Arch at sunrise was worth getting up in the dark. The sun came up right under the arch and turned the whole canyon gold. Then Delicate Arch — three miles, my legs were tired, but the arch at sunset was worth it."
**Wayne:** "I stayed on the rock. Good view, no walking."
**Journal, Day 2:** Best day so far. Sunrise under Mesa Arch, then all day in Arches. Delicate Arch is smaller than I thought from photos, and bigger than I thought when you stand under it. Feet hurt, in a good way.

## Day 3 — Monday Dec 22, 2025: Moab → Capitol Reef → Scenic Byway 12 → Bryce
**Drive:** about 4.5–5 hours. **Stay:** Best Western Plus Ruby's Inn, near Bryce Canyon.

**Spots, in order:**
- 07:30–10:00 Check out early, drive about 2.5 hours west to Torrey, gateway to Capitol Reef.
- 10:00–12:00 Capitol Reef, Fruita Historic District on UT-24. Fruita Schoolhouse, old orchards, cinnamon rolls at Gifford House if open, old petroglyphs on the rock.
- 12:00–16:30 Turn onto Scenic Byway 12 no later than 12:30pm. Over snow mountains and red-rock ridges, including the Hogback. Arrive near Bryce well before dark at 5:10pm. Stay in Bryce Canyon City or Tropic, not Torrey — most winter services in Torrey are closed.

**Where to eat:** Breakfast in Moab before leaving. Lunch in Fruita — cinnamon rolls if Gifford House is open, otherwise picnic sandwiches. Dinner near Bryce — at Ruby's Inn / Bryce Canyon City, winter hours change, so check on arrival. Pack snacks for Highway 12, there is a long stretch with no shops and no phone signal.

**Why stay here:** Ruby's Inn is right by the Bryce entrance, so Day 4 sunrise is a short drive, not a long cold drive in the dark. Staying near Bryce, not Torrey, is the point — Torrey is behind them, Bryce is ahead.

**Photo (V1):** Panel 3 — Luna in the snow by the Fruita schoolhouse, Wayne once on the wooden fence on the right, Scenic Byway 12 sign behind. Note: the connected strip has a tiny white shape under Wayne on the fence that might be a second tiny Wayne — check and fix in V2.

**Vlog script, Day 3 — Luna:** "Capitol Reef was quiet — a little schoolhouse, old apple trees in the snow, and cinnamon rolls. Then Highway 12. It goes up over a mountain, down into a red canyon, up again. I kept saying 'wow' and then forgetting to take a photo."
**Wayne:** "I liked the schoolhouse. Small. Like me."
**Journal, Day 3:** Longest feeling day, even though the drive was shorter than Day 1. Highway 12 is the prettiest road so far. Cold now — Bryce is high up, 8,000–9,000 feet. Put on every warm layer tonight.

## Day 4 — Tuesday Dec 23, 2025: Bryce Canyon → Springdale (Zion)
**Drive:** about 1h45–2h direct, Bryce → Springdale via SR-9. **Stay in the plan:** Hyatt Place St. George / Convention Center — CHECK, see below.

**Spots, in order:**
- 07:15–11:30 Bryce Canyon. Coldest stop. Heavy layers, microspikes. Sunrise Point, sunrise on snow-covered red hoodoos. Then Queen's Garden Trail down into the amphitheater, out and back — the Wall Street part of Navajo Loop is closed in winter, so do not go that way.
- 12:00–15:00 Lunch, then drive to Springdale. Direct via SR-9 through the tunnel, US-89 and SR-12. No detour in the story for last year.
- 15:30–17:00 Check in. If there is time, an easy golden-hour walk on the Pa'rus Trail inside Zion Canyon.

**Where to eat:** Breakfast near Bryce before sunrise, quick and warm. Lunch on the road to Springdale. Dinner in Springdale — Oscar's Cafe on Zion Park Boulevard, Tex-Mex, burgers and burritos, open daily, or Zion Pizza & Noodle Co., pizza and pasta in an old church. Note: Bit & Spur is dinner only and in winter is Thursday–Sunday only — Dec 23, 2025 was a Tuesday, so do not plan on Bit & Spur that night.

**Why stay here — CHECK:** The plan lists overnight as Springdale, UT, but the hotel as Hyatt Place St. George. St. George is about 41–42 miles / 45–60 minutes from Zion's South Entrance at Springdale. That is about 1 hour to St. George on the evening of Dec 23, plus about 1 hour back to Zion on the morning of Dec 24 — about 2 extra hours versus staying in Springdale. St. George may be on purpose — bigger town, maybe cheaper — but if the plan meant to sleep in Springdale, Night 4 needs a Springdale hotel instead. V1 does NOT claim a St. George price or availability. Flagged, not fixed.

**Photo (V1):** Panel 4 — Luna in the snow among the Bryce hoodoos, Wayne once on a rock on the right.

**Vlog script, Day 4 — Luna:** "Bryce at sunrise — white snow on red rocks, like someone painted it. Queen's Garden was slippery, so we went slow, with spikes on our shoes. Then down to Zion, and it got warmer and warmer. Pa'rus Trail at sunset, river on one side, cliffs on the other."
**Wayne:** "Bryce was cold. Zion was better. I stayed on the warm rock."
**Journal, Day 4:** Coldest morning, warmest evening. That is the whole trip in one day. Bryce hoodoos in snow are my favourite photo so far. Tomorrow is the last day — Zion in the morning, Horseshoe Bend, then home.

## Day 5 — Wednesday Dec 24, 2025: Zion morning → Horseshoe Bend → Phoenix
**Drive:** about 7.25 hours total, with stops. **Stay:** Home in Phoenix.

**Spots, in order:**
- 08:00–11:00 Zion National Park, morning. In the story, drive your own car into the canyon — no shuttle Dec 20–24. Hike one of these: Scout Lookout from Stop #6, The Grotto, 3.6 miles round trip, about 2.5–3 hours, big canyon view, no permit, no chains; or Riverside Walk from Stop #9, flat, about 1.5 hours. Skip The Narrows in December — the water is freezing.
- 11:30–13:30 Lunch in Springdale, then drive south via Kanab to Page, AZ, about 2 hours 15 minutes.
- 13:30–14:45 Horseshoe Bend, just south of Page. $10 per car parking, National Park pass does not work. Walk the flat 1.5-mile round trip sandy path, about 1 hour total. Early afternoon sun is best for photos of the Colorado River bend.
- 14:45–19:15 Drive home south on US-89 through Flagstaff, about 4.5 hours. Home in Phoenix about 7:30pm.

**Where to eat:** Breakfast in Springdale — MeMe's Cafe, crepes and breakfast, or Oscar's Cafe. Lunch in Springdale before leaving, or a quick stop in Page — Big John's Texas BBQ on South Lake Powell Boulevard for brisket, or Slackers, 635 Elm St, Page, burgers and ice cream next door. Dinner at home in Phoenix — Christmas Eve, last night of the trip.

**Why stay here:** Home. No hotel on Night 5. The drive home is split — Zion in the morning, Horseshoe Bend at lunch, then a straight drive south — so no one has to drive 7.5 hours in one go while tired.

**Photo (V1):** Panel 5 of the connected strip — Luna and Wayne TOGETHER on the cliff at Horseshoe Bend, Luna normal size, Wayne once beside her, car heading home to Phoenix on the right. The separate big Day 5 close-up is NOT used in V1 — Luna was too big in it.

**Vlog script, Day 5 — Luna:** "Last morning in Zion, then Horseshoe Bend — the river makes a huge U-turn below us, and it looks like a postcard. Wayne and I sat on the edge together and just looked. Then home. Five days, five parks, plus Monument Valley and Horseshoe Bend. Done."
**Wayne:** "I sat next to Luna. That is the whole vlog."
**Journal, Day 5:** Trip done. Five parks in five days. Favourite moments: Mesa Arch sunrise, Delicate Arch sunset, Highway 12, Bryce in snow, Horseshoe Bend with Wayne next to me. Tired, happy, home for Christmas Eve.
