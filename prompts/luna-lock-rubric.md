## Rubric change — automatic rejection

Added to the QC rubric in `CHARACTER-BIBLE-AND-IDENTITY-LOCK.md`, section 0 and section 7.

Any change to Luna from the Prompt 5 picture, in any frame, is an automatic fail, regardless of total score. That includes:
- eyes closing, half-closing, changing colour or shape
- face widening or narrowing, cheeks enlarging
- fur going white, lavender, or dark
- ears changing colour or pattern
- pendant changing colour or shape
- pose changing

A clip that changes Luna cannot score 90+, cannot be accepted, and cannot be "corrected" by freezing — it must be regenerated with Luna locked, or the Luna frames must be replaced with the exact Prompt 5 Luna.

This rule outranks every other rubric category. Luna lock first, then everything else.
