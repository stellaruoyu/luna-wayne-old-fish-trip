## Extra rules — must pass every time
- **R1. Wayne's eyepatch, EVERY panel:** exactly ONE brown eyepatch, on the
  left side of the picture when Wayne faces us, in **every** panel — Day 1,
  Day 2, Day 3, Day 4, **and Day 5**. Never two, never flipped, never
  hidden. A back view on Day 5 does NOT count — Wayne must face the camera
  on Day 5 so his eyepatch can be seen.
- **R2. Wayne's eye:** his open eye is BRIGHT GOLDEN YELLOW — not brown,
  not dark, not green.
- **R3. Old Fish is a fish:** a cute silver-blue robot fish in his bowl.
  Never a cat, never a bunny, never ugly or scary.
- **R4. Same style:** Luna and Wayne look like they belong in the same
  picture — same soft chibi style. Luna's ears hang down, Luna is normal
  size, never a giant close-up.
- **R5. Days in order:** captions say Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Day 4, Day 5 in
  order, each once, with the right date and place. No invented numbers.
- **R6. Voices must be clean:** every voice in a video must be clear,
  easy to understand, and not glitchy or weird. No voice that makes words
  hard to hear. Test each voice alone, on its own lines, before it goes
  into the video. If a voice sounds weird, change the voice, do not keep it.
- **R7. Always a rubric, always change it:** there must always be a
  rubric, and the work must change until it passes it. When a score shows
  a fail, fix the work, update the rubric with what was learned, then
  score again.
- **R8. Wayne is NOT grumpy:** Wayne is a friendly cat who cares about
  his friends — in the vlog he worries "Do not drop my bowl" about Old
  Fish. He is never a grumpy, sarcastic, or grumpy-navigator character.
  Do not write him that way, even if a review suggests it. Luna is the
  excited explorer — curious, energetic, affectionate, sometimes silly.
  Old Fish is the little scientist — logical, accidentally funny, and
  obsessed with numbers, and he can be the one who calculates the trip
  costs. Give each of them different feelings on different days — Luna
  should not have the same face on Days 1–4 — and give them little
  interactions, not just posing for pictures.
- **R9. One fixed voice per character, never changes:** each character
  has ONE voice, the same in every line, every day, and every version.
  The voice must not change in the middle of a video, and it must not
  change from one version to the next, unless Stella says to change it.
  Locked cast: Narrator = Aria, Luna = Paloma, Wayne = Miles, Old Fish
  = Caitlin. Same speed, same cast, every time. If a voice sounds
  different on one line, re-record that line with the locked voice.
- **R10. Never repeat the same scene multiple times:** the same clip,
  the same footage, or the same movement must not play over and over.
  No looping one short clip to fill a long line. Every turn must use
  different footage from the turn before it. If a line is longer than
  one clip, use a different clip for the rest of it, never the same
  clip again.
- **R11. Old Fish can NEVER be luggage:** Old Fish is not allowed to
  just sit in his bowl while the others do everything. He must be part
  of the trip — he speaks, he calculates the costs, he reacts, and he
  gets his own close-ups. If a version leaves him sitting there doing
  nothing, it fails, even if everything else is right.
- **R12. When one person speaks, only that person's mouth moves:**
  when Luna speaks, only Luna's mouth moves; when Wayne speaks, only
  Wayne's mouth moves; when Old Fish speaks, only Old Fish moves. The
  others do not move their mouths. The picture must frame the speaker
  so the others' mouths are not moving on screen.
- **R13. No zooming in on the same scene for different people:** never
  crop-zoom into the same panel to frame a different speaker. Each turn
  shows the full scene. A zoomed-in crop of the same picture is not a
  new scene — it is the same scene, closer up. If the scene is the
  same, show it whole.
- **R14. The person on screen must be the person speaking:** never show
  a mouth moving while a different person's voice is speaking. The
  moving mouth on screen and the voice you hear must belong to the same
  character. If a clip shows Luna's mouth moving while Wayne's voice
  speaks, or anyone's mouth moving while Old Fish's voice speaks, the
  video fails — even if the voices and the pictures are each correct
  on their own.
- **R15. Old Fish must be heard AND seen speaking, every day must have
  talking:** Old Fish's lines must actually be in the video, with his
  mouth moving when he says them — not just written in the script. His
  "fish-stick conditions" line, his "one hundred fish bowls" line, and
  his final tally must all be heard in the video. And every day must
  have talking in it — no silent days. A day with only moving pictures
  and no voices is not a finished day.

| | Design 1 | Design 2 |

| **Total** | /20 | /20 |

*Plus: one honest weakness per design.*

## Rubric 4.0 — The Artist's Scorecard 🎨 (new, from the outside review)

Rubric 2.0 checks whether things are correct. This scorecard checks
whether the artwork is interesting and feels like a real adventure, not
just five postcards. Keep all R1–R12 and Option B — this is added on top,
not instead of them.

| Art check | Max | V2 score |
|---|---|---|
| Character consistency (same look, same eyepatch side, ears down) | /15 | 10 |
| Character personality (Luna excited, Wayne friendly not grumpy, Old Fish funny with numbers) | /15 | 8 |
| Visual storytelling (they do things, feel things, talk to each other — not just pose) | /15 | 9 |
| Composition and camera angles (mix big landscapes, small panels, speech bubbles, travel stamps — not the same rectangle every time) | /10 | 7 |
| Beautiful and distinct scenery (characters smaller sometimes, so the mountains, arches and canyons feel enormous) | /10 | 9 |
| Actual animation (blinking, ear moves, tail moves, Fish swimming and bubbles, falling snow, moving clouds, moving car scenery — not just a camera pan) | /10 | 4 |
| Voice acting and sound design | /10 | — (not verified by the reviewer; V2 voices are clean, tested alone) |
| Emotional connection | /10 | 6 |
| Originality and creativity | /5 | 4 |
| **Total** | **/90** | **57 (63%)** |

*Target: 90% or higher, plus passing all mandatory rules from Rubric 2.0.
V2's 57/90 above is the starting score from the review of the OLD 64-second
video (the v6 video, before V2). Two of that review's notes are already
fixed in V2 and are NOT true of V2 any more: Wayne facing away on Day 5,
and Luna's ears upright — V2 Day 5 faces the camera, and Luna's ears are
down in every panel. What V2 still needs, for the next version: different
feelings on Luna's face each day, more personality and interaction, bigger
scenery, Christmas feeling (scarves, warm drinks, a Christmas surprise on
Day 5, Dec 24), a memorable ending, and real small animation.*

## Option C: The 10-Second Checklist ⚡ (fastest)

A design passes only if you can answer YES to all of these:

- [ ] I can tell which day it is in under 10 seconds
- [ ] I can point to Luna, Wayne, and Old Fish, and they all look right
- [ ] I know what it costs and where to eat and stay
- [ ] It doesn't look boring

*If any box is unchecked, the design goes back for one more round.*
